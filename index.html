Welcome!

Walker Mobile Notary provides professional mobile notary & loan signing services for individuals, families, businesses, attorneys, commercial real estate professionals, property managers, and healthcare facilities throughout the Santa Clarita Valley, San Fernando Valley, Los Angeles County, Ventura County, and surrounding areas.


Services include general notarization, acknowledgments, jurats, powers of attorney, trusts, estate planning, affidavits, commercial real estate documents, loan signings, hospital / senior care visits, and apostille assistance.


We are committed to providing professional, confidential, punctual, detail-oriented service with integrity, ensuring that every client receives a dependable notarization experience.
