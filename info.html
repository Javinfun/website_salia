Information

Welcome to Walker Mobile Notary. We provide professional, reliable mobile notary services with the convenience of meeting you at your preferred location. Whether your notarization is planned in advance or time-sensitive, our goal is to provide a smooth, efficient, and professional experience from scheduling through completion.

Services We Offer

Walker Mobile Notary provides professional mobile notary services for individuals, families, businesses, and professionals throughout our service area.

Services include acknowledgments, jurats, affidavits, powers of attorney, estate planning documents, real estate and loan documents, and a wide range of general notarization needs.

Same-day, evening, weekend, and after-hours appointments may be available based on scheduling and location.

What to Bring

To help ensure a smooth and efficient appointment, please have the following available:

The document(s) requiring notarization Acceptable identification for each signer Any additional individuals required for your document or notarization, when applicable

Please do not sign documents in advance when the notarization requires the signature to be completed in the presence of the notary.

Documents should be complete and ready for notarization before the appointment. Walker Mobile Notary is unable to select, prepare, complete, or provide legal advice regarding your documents.

Scheduling

Appointments may be requested by phone, email, or through our website.

When requesting an appointment, please provide your preferred date and time, general appointment location, and the number of signers requiring notarization. This allows us to confirm availability and provide applicable travel and service information before your appointment.

Same-day and urgent appointment requests are welcome and accommodated whenever availability permits.

Business Hours
